Section 04 • Official Rules

TOURNAMENT FORMAT & MATCH STRUCTURE

Preserving the exact official format defined by Mentor & Mascot Indian Film Federation (MMIFF). No alterations made.

✅
Official Rule Specification: Matches are played as 100 balls per inning (structured as 20 overs). 15 league-stage matches will be played across Days 1–6. The top four ranked teams advance. Qualifier 1: Rank #1 vs Rank #3. Qualifier 2: Rank #2 vs Rank #4. Both winners battle in the Grand Championship Final on Day 8.
Phase 1 • Days 1–6
ROUND-ROBIN LEAGUE STAGE
All 6 franchise teams compete in high-tempo 100-ball / 20-over clashes. Points table rank determined by wins, net run-rate (NRR), and boundary counts.
